Meeting people, safely

Dating & connection

Hum Tum doesn't run its own matching system — instead, here's how to date safely and an icebreaker generator to borrow when you're stuck.

  • Meet public, first Choose a busy public place for early dates, and stay there until you're comfortable.
  • Tell a friend Share your plans, location, and the person's profile with someone you trust.
  • Video chat first A quick call before meeting helps confirm the person is who they say they are.
  • Protect your info Hold off on sharing your home address, workplace, or financial details.
  • Trust your gut If something feels off, it's okay to leave or cancel — no explanation required.
  • Know your local scene Apps like HER, Grindr, Taimi, Lex, and OkCupid all have LGBTQ+-specific spaces — try a few and see what fits.
